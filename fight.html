Kaiju Battle

One pilot, one station. Three AI teammates fill the others. The kaiju has 10,000 HP. Ordinary hits chip it; the Hyper Beam is what kills it.

Defense: block when the kaiju targets your station.
Attack: a good block by anyone calls out a move. If it's one of yours, press it fast, then play it.
Combos: landed attacks in a row open the kaiju's weak point for a short time.
Charge: tap the charge button whenever you're free. Teammates charge too.
Fire into the open weak point at 60%+. Damage climbs steeply with charge: fire early for a scratch, or hold for a kill shot.
