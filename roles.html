Kaiju Battle

You're the sword arm. You can't see the kaiju's guard; only the Head can, and they call it out. Attack whenever you want.

Your three moves hit HIGH, MID or LOW. Hit where the kaiju isn't guarding and you land; hit into the guard and you're blocked and locked out for a moment.
Head says is the Head pilot calling the guard, incoming attacks and openings. They're a little late and sometimes wrong.
Landed hits make the kaiju reel. Hit again while it's reeling to build a combo. Combo of 4 opens the weak point.
Legs set range: close means bigger windows and damage. Left arm blocks; if they're slow you get a last-chance block.
Charge whenever you're free. Fire needs all four pilots pressing together, weak point open, 60%+.
